Meta-T Research Platform 1.1

Research Participation Consent

Purpose
This web application is part of a behavioural research study examining cognitive processes during gameplay.
What We Collect
  • Gameplay actions (key presses, timing, piece placements)
  • Game performance metrics (scores, lines cleared, levels)
  • Session timestamps and duration
  • Questionnaire data from the following questionnaires: CORGIS and NASA-TLX (both freely available online). Short questions on your experience will appear between each short Tetris game, and the full questionnaires will also appear at the end of the study.
  • Demographic data: Handedness, Experience with Tetris, gender
Data Handling
  • Your data is identified only by an anonymous participant ID
  • No personally identifiable information is collected
  • Data may be used for academic research and publications
  • Data is transmitted securely and stored on research servers
Voluntary Participation
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may close this page at any time to decline.
